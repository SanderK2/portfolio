## About Naturity
Naturity was a company that produced natural handmade cosmetics. The products they sold were specifically made from natural and organic ingredients, with a focus on making the products usable by people with sensitive skin. Initially they only sold their products through the Etsy platform, but decided later that they wanted their own fully independent webstore. They reached out to me and through the course of two years I was responsible for making and maintaining their online webstore and blog. The website underwent two major versions, the first one being made with Vue.js and the second one being made with Next.js. 

The website included the following pages:
* Homepage
* Products page
    * Product category page
    * Individual product page
* About page
* Blog overview page
    * Individual blog page
* Contact page
* Retailers page

## From idea to reality
In late 2022 I started to work with Naturity UB to make their first standalone webstore. They had previously been selling their products on platforms like Etsy, and via email and phone. After a short while, they wanted to expand further, and make a dedicated webstore where they sold their products. This was to reach more customers and exert a greater control over the platform where they sold their products.

The head of the company was my brother, and knowing my experience with making websites, he asked me to build their first webstore. They had already chosen a platform for conducting payments and managing inventory, Shopify, so it was up to me to build a front-end that integrated Shopify's functions into it, along with other neccessary pages.
